I'm a co-founder and member of technical staff at [Flourish](https://flourishlabs.ai/). We are a mix of AI researchers and neuroscientists tackling one of the hardest problems in AI: human-level intelligence and learning with human-level efficiency.

Previously, I was at Meta for 13 years. From 2020-2025, I was a research lead in the Neural Interfaces Group where I helped create the [Meta Neural Band](https://www.youtube.com/live/D97ILdUbYww?si=ClCg_DACoSjyMT4g&t=3134). This is the first neural interface to achieve out-of-the-box generalization across people, and the first to ship as a consumer product. I co-founded and led research behind text/language production using [wrist electromyography](https://www.meta.com/emerging-tech/emg-wearable-technology) (EMG), some of which is published in [Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-09255-w) and [NeurIPS](https://ai.meta.com/blog/open-sourcing-surface-electromyography-datasets-neurips-2024).

Before that, I worked on [vision](https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.05085), [reinforcement learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.04054), and [ML systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/1811.09886) at Meta AI (FAIR).

I have been lucky to have lived in SF, NYC, and London during my 20s. I currently live in NYC.
