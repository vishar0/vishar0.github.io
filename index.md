I'm a co-founder and member of technical staff at [Flourish](https://flourishlabs.ai/). We are a mix of AI researchers and neuroscientists building toward an artificial general learner: AI that learns continually and autonomously with human-level sample efficiency, and operates in real time within human-level energy efficiency.

Previously, I worked on a range of AI problems at Meta. I helped develop the [Meta Neural Band](https://www.youtube.com/live/D97ILdUbYww?si=ClCg_DACoSjyMT4g&t=3134), co-founding and leading the research effort on text input from [wrist electromyography](https://www.meta.com/emerging-tech/emg-wearable-technology) (EMG), with work published in [Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-09255-w) and [NeurIPS](https://ai.meta.com/blog/open-sourcing-surface-electromyography-datasets-neurips-2024). I also worked on [vision](https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.05085), [reinforcement learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.04054), and [ML systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/1811.09886) at Meta AI (FAIR).

I have been lucky to have lived in SF, NYC, and London during my twenties. I like to tell myself it's given me some distance from Bay Area groupthink. I currently live in NYC.
